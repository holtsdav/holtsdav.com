Rogue Color

Switch colors and survive for as long as you can.

Rogue Color is a black-and-white survival action game. Match your color to your enemies, collect upgrades, and try to beat your own high score.

Open on itch.ioGitHubSend feedback →

Features

My first game project.

Rogue Color was built with Godot. The prototype is available for Windows, macOS, and Linux on itch.io, and the project is also on GitHub.

01

Switch colors

Press E to switch between black and white. Your color determines which enemies you can defend yourself against.

02

Rising difficulty

From the third minute onward, enemy health and damage double with every additional minute.

03

Game jam project

Rogue Color was my first game and was created in seven days for the Theana Game Jam theme Black & White.
