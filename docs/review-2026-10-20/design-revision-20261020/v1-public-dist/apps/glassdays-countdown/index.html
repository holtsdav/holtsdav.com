01 / THE IDEA

A date is useful.
A moment is personal.

Some dates deserve more than a row in a calendar. Glassdays gives them a place of their own, with a photo, an emoji, and a countdown you can keep close.

It is designed for small, everyday glances: a collection of upcoming plans in the app, or a widget on your Home Screen.

02 / IN THE APP

Make it your own.

A few of the details inside Glassdays.

01 / PERSONALIZE

A countdown with character.

Choose a photo and emoji for each moment. Pin the ones you want to see first, and make the collection feel like yours.

02 / ORGANIZE

Give your plans a place.

Group countdowns in collections, from holidays to the things you follow. Keep your next moments together without losing the ones that matter most.
