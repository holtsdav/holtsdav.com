Mute On Location

Automatically mute at saved locations.

Mute On Location mutes your Mac’s current audio output when you arrive at a saved location. Manage locations, radii, and automation directly from the menu bar app.

Features

Stay muted without having to remember.

Mute On Location is available for Mac on the App Store. Set up your saved places and choose how the app checks for arrivals.

01

Save locations and radii

Add places such as your office, school, or library and choose a suitable radius for each one.

02

Mute automatically

Smart Monitoring detects when you reach a saved location and mutes the selected audio output.

03

Data stays on your Mac

Saved locations and settings remain in the local app container. The app does not create a travel or location history.
