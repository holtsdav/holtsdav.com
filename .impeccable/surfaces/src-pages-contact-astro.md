# Contact

Scope: /contact/. Mode: Operate. A simple way to email David directly or send a message from the site.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Two direct ways to get in touch, with no additional choices or promotional content.

OWN-WORLD: Inherit the portfolio's dark background, Arial typography, pale text, blue accent, and existing header and footer.

STORY: Visitors choose their email app or fill in their name, email, and message. Delivery feedback stays beside the form.

FIRST VIEWPORT: A compact Contact heading; email option on the left, form on the right. On mobile, stack email above the form. Send message is the primary action.

FORM: Precisely specified narrow addition; no concept seed. Interaction: clear pending, success, verification, and retry states with preserved input on failure. No decorative animation.
